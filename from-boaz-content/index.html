Who we are

Goodah is a global non-profit organization that utilizes the mutual relationship between consumers and companies to promote positive advancements in under-privileged areas.

The Goodah platform promotes consumer awareness for company brands that give back to society.

Goodah increases ROI for affiliated companies through goodwill marketing in the short term, and creation of new local and global markets over the long term.

Goodah allocates its resources via affiliated NGO's to create better health education and job creation.

Our Values

Choose

The Goodah platform enables consumers to make a difference in society by encouraging them to choose Goodah-affiliated products.

Buy

Goodah-affiliated products are clearly labeled as such, and include the percentage of marketing budget the company allocates to Goodah.

Change

New consumer markets are created in those regions where Goodah is helping to bring about positive economic and social growth.

Goodah's allocation of Funds

Goodah directs its proceeds to NGO's with proven performance records in the areas of Health improvement, Education and Creation of Jobs within the communities in which they operate.

04

Affiliated NGO's promote social and economic change

Goodah-funded NGO's operate in developed communities around the world. They focus on 3 key elements: Health, education and the creation of jobs. Thus, they help create the foundation for self-generated economic and social prosperity.
